# Sahanaya

> "It's Okay Not To Be Okay."

**Sahanaya** is an open-source digital mental wellness platform developed for the **Leo Club of Pannipitiya Paradise, Sri Lanka** (District 306 A2). The platform is designed to provide free, confidential mental wellness support for Sri Lankan students, employees, and healthcare workers, offering resources, self-assessments, relaxation techniques, and directing users toward professional mental health services.

*Important Disclaimer: Sahanaya is a digital support resource and does NOT replace professional medical or psychological services. In case of an emergency, users must be directed to professional medical help immediately.*

---

## 🌟 Tech Stack

### Frontend
- **Framework**: Next.js 15 (App Router)
- **UI & Logic**: React 19 & TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS
- **Animations**: Framer Motion
- **Icons**: Lucide Icons
- **Theme**: Soft, calm, mental wellness theme with full Dark Mode support (`next-themes`)

### Backend
- **Framework**: Node.js & Express with TypeScript
- **Database**: MongoDB & Mongoose
- **Security**: Helmet, Express Rate Limit, Express Mongo Sanitize, CORS
- **Authentication**: JWT & placeholders for Google OAuth / Anonymous Guest Access

## 🏥 Emergency Warning
Every page of the application includes a clear warning banner emphasizing that Sahanaya is not a clinical service. In case of an emergency, users are directed to local national helpline contacts (e.g., Sumithrayo, 1926 National Mental Health Helpline).
